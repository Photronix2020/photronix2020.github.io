Compass Strip

A compass across the top of your screen that turns exactly with your character and lines up with your view, so a marker on the strip sits right above the place it marks. Markers are smoothed, so icons glide into place instead of jumping around.

Turn around and watch the markers

What it does

Your world map, straight ahead

Smooth, never twitchy

Every marker eases into place, so icons glide instead of jumping around. The strip itself turns 1:1 with you and never lags.

Matches your view

The edges of the strip are the edges of your screen. It follows your field of view, so a marker sits right above the spot it marks.

Works with your map addons

Pins from HandyNotes, MapUtils, GatherMate2, TomTom, RareScanner and other map addons show up, with the same icons as the world map.

Everything that matters

Quest givers and turn-ins, flight points, points of interest, your waypoint, your corpse, and party and raid members.

Name and distance

Face a marker to see its name and distance under it, or point at any marker with the mouse.

Two looks

The full-width Strip, or the Nordic skin: a short monochrome bar with knotwork ends. It fades out in dungeons, where the game hides your direction.

In game

Why another compass?

I tried the compass addons out there and kept running into the same few problems, so Compass Strip was built to fix them.

CompassHUD

Jittery. It spreads markers evenly over a fixed angle and moves them in steps with no smoothing, so icons hop around while you walk and turn. Compass Strip smooths every marker and places it by your real view.

Orbit: Compass

In my testing it didn't play nicely next to other addons. Compass Strip only reads what your map addons already show and never changes them, so they keep working the way they did.

CHUD (Compass H.U.D.) Forever

It doesn't show all the markers on your world map, and it has far too many options. Compass Strip shows everything on your map, with a short list of settings that all fit in one small window.

Type /cstrip or click the minimap button for options: smoothing, view width, marker range, size, height and skin. World map icons from other addons show up after you open the map once in a zone; they are remembered after that.
