Craft your way up a leather chain

A leatherworker at skill 120 with a few things in their bags. Pick a recipe, then click the blocks (or Next) to craft. Skin a beast or visit a vendor on the right to change what you carry.

Your bags

What it does

The whole chain, one click per step

See the chain

The recipe at the top and everything it needs below: what you have, what to buy, and what you can craft from other materials, like light leather into medium into gloves.

Click to craft

Every craft block is a button that makes as many as your materials allow. Next always points at the step to do now.

Leveling guide built in

What the leveling guides recommend for your skill, for every crafting profession, pinned at the top of your picks. One click charts it.

Best picks

Your recipes ranked by skill-up chance and profit. Slide toward skill-ups or gold to choose what matters more. Choose whether materials in your bags and bank count as free.

From my bags

What you can make right now from what you carry, and what you're close to, like "only need 2 Salt".

Real prices

Auction prices from Auctionator and vendor prices remembered from your visits, so you know what the missing parts cost.

Every profession

Leatherworking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Alchemy, Engineering, Cooking, First Aid and smelting. It docks beside the profession window.

In game

Type /pflow to show or hide the chart. Skill-up chances are estimates from the recipe color: orange always, yellow usually, green now and then, gray never. The demo uses sample recipes and prices.
