What it does

Three colors, zero setup

Red: cast now

Turns red inside your spell queue window, so your press fires the instant the ability is ready.

Green: next up

The spell that turns red next, shown at the same time as red or yellow, counting down so you're ready.

Yellow: wait

Let Auto Shot land, let your wand shoot, or hold your damage for threat. Gold marks a big cooldown that's ready: your call.

Combat halo

Glowing arcs around your character with your spells on the ring, power and pet health rings, range icon and a DPS gauge.

Every class

Rotations for all nine classes and tanks, with racials, forms, stances and pets, adapting the moment you learn something new.

Learns from your fights

Fight lengths, damage per cast and your reaction time, to plan the next pull better. HOW shows what following the cues is worth.

Groups and PvP

Waits for the tank and holds your damage near the threat limit. Against players it switches to burst, slows and earlier defenses.

Your own bars too

The same colors glow on Blizzard, Bartender4, Dominos and ElvUI buttons, so you can play from your usual bars.

Your call

Pass over a set-up step (pet, Hunter's Mark, an opener) for another spell and ForeCast carries on from the spell you chose.
