Somebody ganked you? They go on a WANTED poster the moment you die. Get them back and KILLED gets stamped across it in big red letters. When an enemy player shows up, it shouts WATCH OUT!

What it does

Every grudge, on paper

WANTED poster

Portrait (3D while they're in sight, else their race), name, level, race, class, guild, how often they've killed you, where and when, and a well-earned reward.

KILLED stamp

Kill an enemy player and their poster comes up stamped KILLED. Get someone who killed you: Revenge for 3 deaths!

WATCH OUT!

A big red warning and a sound when an enemy player appears. Repeat killers, past victims and anyone targeting you get called out.

Two lists

They killed me and I killed them: level, race and class, guild, kills, last place and time. Sortable, with a poster for every name.

Your guild

Guildmates with the addon share ganks and kills, with a lifetime leaderboard. Every gank is announced in guild chat with the zone.

Try it without dying

Simulate a gank, a kill or a guildmate's message from the settings, so you can see everything before your first real fight.

In game

Who killed you comes from the game's death recap; if that's missing, the addon blames the enemy player who last had you targeted. Mobs don't get posters. Questions and bugs: CurseForge comments.
