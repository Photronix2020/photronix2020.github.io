What it does

Nobody gets lost, nobody gets left behind

Party arrows

Beside each portrait: green near, yellow midway, red far, with distance, area and what they're doing (bank, trainer, mailbox, AFK...).

Shared quest grid

One line per objective, a column per player in class colors. Done, not in their log, offline: all clearly marked, never guessed.

Navigation arrow

Points to your quest, a rally point, the party leader, or your corpse while you're a ghost.

Party quests

See quests your party has that you don't and ask for a share with one click. Follow the leader's quest focus.

Announcements

Optional party chat lines like "Raptors 2/8" that everyone sees, even without PackTrack.

Zone and Near

Track your zone's quests in one click, or sort nearest first with the distance to each.

In game

Every party member whose quest progress you want to see needs PackTrack too; the arrows only need your copy. Questions and bugs: CurseForge comments.
